# 📜 CosmoLand server rules

::: info Important
Not knowing the rules does not exempt a player from responsibility. The administration may change the rules without prior notice.
:::

## General rules

### Insults, spam and threats
Insults toward relatives, spam, excessive caps, threats and harassment are prohibited.

### Real-money trading
Selling or transferring accounts, currency or items for real money is prohibited.

### Cheats, bugs and exploits
Using bugs, exploits or third-party software that gives an advantage is prohibited.

### Fraud and advertising
Deceiving players for profit and advertising outside resources without approval are prohibited.

### Griefing and lag machines
Damaging other players' property, creating traps or intentionally causing server lag is prohibited.

### Administration and accounts
Impersonating staff, misleading the administration or using another player's account is prohibited.
