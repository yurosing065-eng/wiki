# Rockets and flights

A rocket is a vehicle for travelling between worlds. It can carry up to four players: one pilot and three passengers.

## Preparing a rocket

1. Assemble the rocket at a workbench.
2. Place it in an open area with enough room around it.
3. Right-click the rocket to board it.
4. Refuel it by using a fuel item on the rocket.

## Controls

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Gain altitude | Space |
| Brake and descend | Shift |
| Choose a planet | Menu after reaching altitude |
| Leave the rocket | Shift |

Once the rocket reaches the required altitude, a destination menu opens. Earth, the Moon and Mars are available.

## Fuel

Rockets can use coal, charcoal, blaze powder, blaze rods and coal blocks. Better fuel items provide more fuel.

::: warning Safety
Do not hit the ground at high speed. A hard landing can damage the pilot, while a strong crash destroys the rocket.
:::

## Planets

- **Earth** — the safe starting world.
- **Moon** — requires fuel and has lower gravity.
- **Mars** — requires more fuel and has different movement conditions.

::: tip Advice
Check everyone's equipment and make sure the rocket has enough fuel before departure.
:::
