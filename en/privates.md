# Private regions

A private region protects a selected area from other players. Its size is determined by the protection radius in blocks.

## Available sizes

| Size | Protection radius | Price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Small region | 8 blocks | 16,000 |
| Medium region | 32 blocks | 64,000 |
| Large region | 64 blocks | 256,000 |

The radius extends from the center of the region in every direction. Choose a size that fits your build before purchasing.

## How to buy a private region

Private regions can be purchased from the seller at the designated shop.

![Private region shop](/privates-shop.png)

Open the purchase menu and hover over an option to see its radius and price.

![Private region purchase menu](/privates-menu.png)

### 32-block private region

The 32-block private region costs **64,000** coins.

![32-block private region](/private-32.png)

### 64-block private region

The 64-block private region costs **256,000** coins.

![64-block private region](/private-64.png)

::: tip Advice
An 8-block radius is usually enough for a small base. Larger builds and farms may need 32 or 64 blocks.
:::
