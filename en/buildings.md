# Server mechanics

Read the [Rockets and flights](./rockets) guide for space travel.

## Oxygen

Oxygen is one of the server's unique mechanics. Prepare a helmet before leaving.

::: warning Important
Keep an eye on your oxygen level.
:::

## Flight

From the Solix rank and above, you can use `/getfly` in your own region.

::: warning Important
When leaving a region where you had access, you have five seconds to land.
:::
