# Useful mods

## Simple Voice Chat

Simple Voice Chat adds proximity voice communication directly to the game.

### Opening the menu

Press **V** to open the menu. You can open settings, create or join a voice group, mute your microphone and check your devices.

![Simple Voice Chat menu](/voice-chat-menu.png)

### Voice chat settings

You can configure volume, microphone gain, noise suppression, activation mode, voice activation threshold, 3D audio, microphone and speaker selection.

![Simple Voice Chat settings](/voice-chat-settings.png)

::: tip Advice
If other players cannot hear you, check the selected microphone, increase the gain and enable noise suppression. Use manual activation when you want to stay quiet.
:::
